✅ Before Donation

  • 💧
    Stay Hydrated

    Drink 3-4 glasses of water before donation

  • 🍽️
    Eat Well

    Have a healthy meal 3 hours before

  • 😴
    Get Rest

    Ensure 6-8 hours of sleep

  • 🚫
    Avoid Alcohol

    No alcohol 24 hours before

💚 After Donation

  • 🧃
    Drink Fluids

    Extra fluids for next 24 hours

  • 🪑
    Rest

    Relax for 10-15 minutes

  • 🏋️
    Avoid Heavy Activity

    No strenuous exercise for 24 hours

  • 🩹
    Keep Bandage

    Leave bandage on for 4-5 hours
